About

Everything you love about eating out, brought home.

Offset Hospitality is born out of our desire to bring terrific food and exceptional service out of the restaurant and into your home. We have spent over ten years in busy, fine dining restaurants, constantly learning and growing our repertoire of dishes, techniques, and ideas. The greatest thing we have learned from our experience is that the happiest guests we ever had were the ones we truly made to feel at home in the restaurant. We now strive to bring this same hospitality to you, allowing our hosts to relax and enjoy the evening with their guests.

FAQ

Common questions.

01How much does a private chef service cost?

Our service begins at $100 per person for parties of two to twenty persons, with a $750 minimum. Sample menus and prices are provided here for reference. We love working with hosts to curate a great menu that fits their budget. Please feel free to get in touch!

02Do I need to clean up?

We are a full service private chef outfit and promise to return your kitchen to you cleaner than we found it! This being said, we do ask that hosts have a clean and clutter free kitchen when we arrive, so that we may get right into preparing and cooking for your event.

03Do you accommodate dietary restrictions and allergies?

We are happy to accommodate dietary restrictions and allergies. We kindly ask that your guests inform you of any such restrictions before the day of the event. We may not be able to accommodate dietary restrictions and allergies we are first informed of on the day of the event.

04How far in advance do I need to make a booking?

We ask that hosts book at least two weeks in advance in order to allow time for a thorough discussion about the menu and the event to take place. However, we often have surprise openings, so if you need to put on a party sooner, please let us know.

05What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

We understand that things come up and plans change. If an event needs to be cancelled or rescheduled prior to five days before the date of the event, hosts will be refunded their entire deposit. If cancellation or rescheduling is necessary within the 5 days before the event, the deposit will be forfeited. In the event of a day of or day before the event cancellation, hosts will be charged the entire quoted price for the event. Full details on the Cancellation Policy page.

Policy

Cancellation Policy

We understand that things come up and plans change. Here's how cancellations and reschedules are handled.

01Deposit

Securing your date requires a deposit of the greater of $350 or 20% of the quoted total.

025+ days before your event

Cancel or reschedule at least five days before the event date, and your full deposit is refunded.

03Within 5 days of your event

Cancellations or reschedules made within five days of the event forfeit the deposit.

04Day-of or day-before cancellations

Cancelling or rescheduling the day of or the day before the event means the full quoted price for the event is charged.

05Guest count

We'll confirm your final guest count five days before the event — that's the number you're charged for, even if fewer guests attend.

06Questions about a booking

Get in touch and we'll walk through your specific date.
